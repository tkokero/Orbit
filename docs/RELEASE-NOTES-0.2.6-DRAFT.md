# Orbit 0.2.6 — draft

Desktop organization, clearer navigation, and simpler Friends. This release is unpublished.

## Library

- Choose covers, landscape banners, or a list, with saved card size and spacing.
- Pin collections and choose their names, icons, and colors.
- Use Ctrl/Shift selection and bulk favorites, tags, collections, and hiding, with Undo.
- See active filters, clearer counts, and useful empty-result actions.
- Keep your place when closing Details or reselecting the current workspace.
- Exclude unverified play-history entries while keeping their statistics for confirmed games.

## Friends and privacy

- Compact account controls and fresh Online/DND presence, with playing friends first.
- Confirm suggested Steam/Discord profile links and change them anytime.
- Optional read-only Discord Friends; Epic remains client-only.
- Hidden game names show **Playing a game** in Rich Presence. Guest mode and sharing off still clear activity.

## Interface and reliability

- Larger Desktop banners, consistent controls, and related actions grouped together.
- A simpler tour with fixed Next/Back controls and grouped steps.
- Working news links and inline photos, clearer artwork proportions, and contextual right-click menus.
- Steam download cards, storage capacity, and clearer connection/retry controls.
- Downloads keeps its queue and filters while Steam controls open.
- Faster login opening from the cached library, without waiting for the full intro. Manual intro choices stay saved.
- A two-second tray unload target when no protected work is active.
- Desktop reopening after updates/login, a hidden startup scene, and preserved settings, themes, artwork, and profile links.

Detailed checks and remaining native playtests stay in the candidate validation records. Publication requires explicit approval.
