# Desktop design direction

Orbit is a game launcher. Game artwork and the user's next action should lead; navigation and utility controls support them.

The dashboard cleanup reference is [Damir Sokolovsky's before/after clip](https://x.com/heydamir/status/2115063463063998736). Apply its clearer hierarchy and restraint rather than copying its finance content or forcing a monochrome theme.

- Retain Orbit's rounded type, comfortable corners, selected themes, and large undistorted game art.
- Use bare, aligned utility symbols; avoid putting every icon inside a second button-shaped circle.
- Reserve strong theme accents for primary actions, selected navigation, and useful state. Keep counts readable and aligned rather than decorating them as badges.
- Keep related actions in horizontal rows, with consistent gaps and natural wrapping. Keep forms, lists, and explicit choices in their appropriate layouts.
- Use a single clear outline for a control. Keep keyboard focus visible and preserve primary-action contrast in every theme.
- Keep hover feedback small and predictable. Avoid icon spinning, routine control glows, and navigation shifting sideways.
- Preserve recognizable launcher symbols, meaningful warnings, privacy explanations, and status. Removing decoration must not remove information.
- Scope this cleanup to Desktop chrome. Controller Home/Library, startup scenes, selected artwork, popup preferences, and functional behavior remain unchanged.

Verify rendered captures at wide/narrow widths, larger scaling, light/dark themes, and reduced motion. Development checks and installs stay hidden.
