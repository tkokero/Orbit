# Quick Start

Open **Settings → About → Quick Start** to personalize Orbit again at any time. Existing choices stay selected.

1. **Appearance:** choose a theme, match the app icon, and set interface motion. Shell icons apply after Restart Orbit.
2. **Startup:** pick an unlocked animation and its sound volume. Use **Preview** to try it. Turning animations off keeps your selected scene.
3. **Library:** choose covers, banners or a list, adjust card size, and show or hide the banner, recents and news. Click the preview to toggle a section. Expand **Libraries** to connect or scan games.
4. **Preferences:** choose Windows startup, tray behavior, discovery, session history and updates. Development builds also offer optional detected-game clipping on startup. Microphone capture stays a separate Clips setting.
5. **Explore:** choose **Take a tour** for the real app controls, **Open library**, or **Set up clipping**.

**Finish later** remembers your step. Closing the guide returns to your previous workspace. Theme, layout and preference changes save when you choose them.

See [clipping](CLIPPING.md) for recording modes, bookmarks, audio and storage.
